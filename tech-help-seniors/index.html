What I can help with

It's not you. Technology is badly explained. If any of these sound familiar, you're in good company.

  • "My phone keeps asking for a code."
  • "I can't find my photos."
  • "I got a strange email. Is it real?"
  • "I forgot my password again."
  • "The Wi-Fi stopped working."
  • "The words on my screen are too small."
  • "I want to video call my grandkids."
  • "Someone called and said my computer has a virus."
  • "My email is full of junk."

Don't see yours? Ask anyway. If it's on your device, we can work it out together.

Questions people ask

Tap a question to see the answer.

Do I need to buy anything?

No. I work with what you already have. If something really does need replacing, I'll tell you honestly and explain your choices.

What if I feel embarrassed to ask?

Please don't. I've taught more than 3,000 older adults, and there are no silly questions. It's not you. Technology is badly explained.

Do you come to my home?

Yes. I visit homes in Franklin and Delaware Counties. If you'd rather not have a visitor, a phone consultation works well too.

Can family members join?

Yes. A son, daughter, friend, or neighbor is welcome, in person or on the phone. A second set of ears can help.

What devices do you help with?

Phones, tablets, computers, and the Wi-Fi that connects them. Also email, passwords, photos, and spotting scams. Not sure if I can help? Just ask.

Can you fix my broken computer?

Honestly, no. I teach and guide. If something is truly broken, I'll help you understand what's going on and point you to the right person to fix it.

Can you help me spot scams?

Yes. This is one of the most useful things we can do together. I'll show you the warning signs in emails, texts, and phone calls, so you know when to stop and not click, call back, or pay.

How do you keep my passwords and information private?

Your passwords stay yours. You type them in yourself, and I won't keep copies of your information. I'll also help you find a safe, simple way to keep track of them.
